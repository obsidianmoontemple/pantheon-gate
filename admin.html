    Energy & Tickets (0071)

    "We need to create an energy system for campaigning and fighting." Energy gates Campaign, Gauntlet, World Boss, Circle Dragon, and Mage Duel attempts — one shared pool per player, since it lives on their Guardian row. PvP uses a separate pool, Tickets, so grinding one kind of fight dry never blocks the other. Both regenerate passively over time (no cron job — the elapsed time since each was last spent is computed fresh whenever it's checked, same as Mage HP). World Boss, Circle Dragon, and Mage Duel keep their existing per-user cooldowns on top of this — the cooldown stops burst-clicking one request, Energy is the budget across a whole session.

    Energy

    Off by default. Turn this on to run a temporary "no cap" event — grants (admin or gift) and regen stop clamping at Max Energy above, so a balance can sit above it. Nothing is lost when you turn it back off; balances above the cap just stop being usable again until you either re-enable this or raise Max Energy to match.

    Move Catalog

    13-point update item 13: every individual move in the game, defined once here — edit a move's stats and it updates everywhere it's assigned below (an affinity baseline, a species's signature move, or a deity's signature override), no more hunting down the same move in three tables. Kind picks the slot a move can fill: Power (no swiftness shave) or Control (hits softer, shaves the defender's Swiftness for the rest of the fight) need an Affinity (used by the Element Wheel); Basic is the universal fallback strike and has none. Archetype (0086) tags what the move is FOR, independent of Kind: Damage (the default — deals its Power as a hit), Support (heals/buffs the caster — set Power to 0 for a pure heal that deals no damage at all), or Tank (defensive — shield_flat works standalone now instead of only riding on a damage roll). A move can also carry a real triggered effect on top of its damage: lifesteal_pct (heal this % of the hit's damage), stun_pct (this % chance to skip the target's next action), crit_bonus_pct (+this many points of crit chance on this hit), burn_flat (ongoing damage/round for the rest of the fight), poison_pct (a second, independent DoT — this % of the target's max Vitality/round), weaken_pct (cuts the target's own Might by this % for the rest of the fight), vulnerability_pct (the target takes this % more damage from everything for the rest of the fight), shield_flat (self-buff — absorbs your own next this-much total damage taken), heal_flat (0086 — heal the caster this flat amount, no damage dealt), or heal_pct (0086 — heal the caster this % of their own max Vitality, no damage dealt). Role is just a label for where a move is meant to be used — nothing reads it, so any move (including a brand new "custom" one) can be assigned anywhere a dropdown below lets you pick one. Particle (0070) picks which burst sprite plays when this move lands in Campaign, Gauntlet, and PvP — leave it "— affinity default —" to keep the automatic look every other move of that affinity already has, or pick a specific one to make this move stand out.

    Free-form Q&A, unlocked once a player has seen the walkthrough. Limit questions per player per day to control cost/misuse; 0 means unlimited.
