    Spawn a new World Boss

    The boss now retaliates on every swing (direct feedback: "it needs to retaliate") — Power is its counter-attack strength; a swing that would meet or exceed an attacker's vitality repels that attack entirely (0 damage that swing, cooldown still applies).
